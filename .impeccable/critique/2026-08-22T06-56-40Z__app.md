## Design Specificity Verdict

The site is authored for this portfolio, not category-interchangeable. The single WaveField, route-specific signals, BPM-based motion, persistent transport, EXIF readouts, and equal-weight Builds/Music/Lens navigation make Snehil's range structural. Specificity weakens below the shell: Music becomes a flat 22-row catalog, Lens becomes a 15,065px one-up mobile stream, and Builds becomes a long sequence after the excellent featured project.

The deterministic CLI scan returned zero static findings. Browser injection succeeded on all five routes at 1440x900 and 390x844. It found the real heading gaps, one unbounded desktop project paragraph, two source-confirmed LCP-priority warnings, and one unresolved Framer Motion warning. Its colored-glow warnings are false positives: ScrollProgressRail and SignalRule are four explicitly documented, intentional Instrument Panel shadow uses. Its `BuildsBuilds` text report is also a false positive: ManifestoHeading carries one sr-only accessible label plus an aria-hidden animated rendering inside one h1. The `/builds` warning names `fere-win.jpeg`, which `projects.ts:39` assigns as `secondaryImage`; that separate Image at `FeaturedProject.tsx:70-76` lacks the `priority` applied to the primary `fere.jpg`.

No reliable human-visible overlay remains open: injection ran in isolated headless tabs and those helper processes were stopped.

## Overall Impression

Strong visual identity and interaction fundamentals. The biggest opportunity is editorial hierarchy, not redesign: keep the Instrument Panel shell and make the catalogs tell visitors where to start. Shipping craft narrowly misses because fast-jump content presence, mobile Lens pacing, semantic collection headings, and two first-viewport LCP hints remain unfinished.

## What's Working

1. Proof is composed rather than appended: Home and the Fere feature pair claims with first-party artifacts and external validation.
2. The three-discipline claim survives every layer: projects, beats, and photographs each use their native metadata and interaction grammar.
3. Accessibility mechanics are unusually deliberate: mobile lightbox focus trapping/restoration passed, empty contact submission focused `#name` and exposed all three inline errors, 320px reflow had no horizontal overflow on any route, and reduced-motion preference matched.

## Priority Issues

### [P2] Fully hidden entrance defaults can make fast jumps look broken
- **Evidence:** programmatic instant jumps briefly produced WaveField-only viewports on Home and mobile Builds. `HomeContent.tsx:20-23`, `ExperienceList.tsx:23,41-45`, `ProjectCard.tsx:122-139,162-190`, and `SkillsMatrix.tsx:23-28,48-53` start meaningful content fully transparent or clipped.
- **Why it matters:** scrollbar drags, restored positions, find-in-page, Page Down, or fast touch scroll can land on apparently empty content.
- **Fix:** keep semantic content visible by default; move the authored reveal into rules, short clip/defocus treatments, or already-visible transforms. Verify wheel, scrollbar drag, browser find, history restoration, and reduced motion.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable animate app`.

### [P2] Mobile Lens loses the contact-sheet concept
- **Evidence:** 390x844 rendered one photo per row and a 15,065px document. `JustifiedGrid.tsx` uses `MIN_PLATE = 260`, forcing `maxPerRow` to one at mobile content width.
- **Why it matters:** visitors cannot quickly compare frames, perceive range, or reach the close of the collection.
- **Fix:** preserve intrinsic ratios, EXIF, lightbox geometry, and large touch targets, but use adaptive mobile packing that permits two landscape frames when geometry allows while keeping portraits legible.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt app/lens`.

### [P2] Music provides 22 equal starts but no authored starting point
- **Evidence:** `MusicPage.tsx` maps all 22 beats through identical TrackRows; the desktop first viewport exposes roughly fifteen peers.
- **Why it matters:** the catalog proves volume but does not tell a recruiter or direct music visitor what best represents the work.
- **Fix:** do not restore descriptions. Nominate one `start here` track or a restrained three-track sequence using the existing row grammar, then retain the full catalog behind a section boundary or disclosure.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill app/music`.

### [P2] Builds is expensive to compare after the featured proof
- **Evidence:** 4,707px desktop and 7,468px mobile; `WorkPage.tsx:19-21,46-74` renders Fere, five full ProjectCards, then five compact cards. `FeaturedProject.tsx:110` also lacks a prose measure cap and measured around 154 characters per line at desktop width.
- **Why it matters:** the strongest recruiter journey peaks immediately, then spends attention on sequential reading rather than comparison.
- **Fix:** keep Fere featured; reuse the compact details pattern for more remaining projects, exposing title, date, and one factual comparison signal. Add the existing body measure token to the featured description.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill app/builds` plus `/impeccable typeset app/builds`.

### [P2] Home and Builds have incomplete heading outlines
- **Evidence:** rendered outlines are h1 directly to company/project h3 headings. HomeContent has `section aria-label="Experience"`; WorkPage has `section aria-label="Projects"`, but neither contains the semantic h2 parent.
- **Why it matters:** screen-reader heading navigation reports a missing level and makes the item groups less understandable.
- **Fix:** preserve company/project titles as h3 and add one sr-only h2 inside each existing labeled collection: Experience in HomeContent and Projects in WorkPage.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden app`.

### [P3] Two first-viewport images lack LCP preload hints
- **Evidence:** the browser console identifies `fere-win.jpeg` on Builds and `dog.jpg` on Reach. The Fere primary `fere.jpg` is priority, but the separate secondary Image rendering `fere-win.jpeg` at `FeaturedProject.tsx:70-76` is not; `app/reach/page.tsx:108-113` renders the first contact image without priority.
- **Why it matters:** either visible image can become the breakpoint-specific LCP candidate without an early preload hint.
- **Fix:** verify production LCP candidates at representative breakpoints, then apply `priority` only to the confirmed first-viewport candidates.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable optimize app/builds app/reach`.

### [P3] Contact recovery copy is generic
- **Evidence:** `ContactForm.tsx:91-99` maps multiple distinct email problems to `Not a valid email address`, including one missing period; `:194-200` uses one message for timeout, offline, and rejected fetch.
- **Why it matters:** the form preserves input correctly, but visitors must infer what to change.
- **Fix:** name the recovery action while retaining the current inline placement, focus behavior, and palette.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify app/reach`.
