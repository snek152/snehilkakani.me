## Overall Impression

A coherent, technically deliberate experience whose highest-value remaining work is accessibility semantics and post-action focus—not a visual redesign.

## What’s Working

- The lightbox is a well-implemented dialog: keyboard navigation, focus trap, Escape, restored trigger focus, meaningful exposure detail, and the repaired backdrop close path.
- Motion preferences are honored end-to-end, including a static WaveField frame under reduced motion.
- The contact handoff is unusually honest and resilient: explicit mailto uncertainty, preserved draft, and recovery actions.

## Priority Issues

- **[P2] Focus is lost after contact submission.** `app/lib/components/contact/ContactForm.tsx`. A keyboard submit leaves `document.activeElement` on `body` instead of moving to the status panel or first recovery control. This breaks the recovery path for keyboard users. **Fix:** focus the status region or its first action once the result replaces the form. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`.
- **[P2] Desktop navigation labels are hover-only and current route semantics are absent.** `app/lib/components/Sidebar.tsx:29-64, 152-165`. The 52px desktop rail renders labels at zero opacity until hover, and nav links lack `aria-current`. **Fix:** expand labels on focus-within and set `aria-current="page"` on the active route. Preserve the documented overlay rail rather than changing its geometry. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`.
- **[P2] Music toggle exposes selected state, not actual playback state.** `app/lib/components/music/TrackRow.tsx:44-49`. `aria-pressed={isActive}` remains true for a paused track whose label says “Play”. **Fix:** express pressed state from `isPlayingRow`, or use an alternative selected-track semantic that does not conflict with the action label. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`.
- **[P3] Project action labels hide their destination type.** `app/lib/components/work/ProjectMeta.tsx`. A PDF, image, video, and live app all announce as a generic project link. **Fix:** distinguish “View case study PDF,” “View project image,” “Watch demo,” and “Open live site.” **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`.

The critique’s visual 19px-link P1 is a false positive: `ProjectMeta` supplies an `::before` hit slop that produces a documented 27×27 target, exceeding WCAG 2.5.8’s 24px minimum. The sidebar’s expanded overlay is also intentional per `DESIGN.md`; its content overlap should not be “fixed” by reflowing the page.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Casey (mobile):** mobile navigation itself is readable and has no horizontal overflow; destination disclosure remains the relevant friction.
