## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored, not templated.** This does not read as vibe-coded work, and the evidence is specific:

- The hero answers the recruiter's four questions as a *data structure* — Currently building / Studying / Based in / Seeking — beside a real photograph and a résumé download. Most portfolios open with an adjective.
- Each surface expresses its own discipline rather than repeating one card grid: `/lens` is a justified contact sheet with real EXIF triplets under each frame; `/music` is a label release sheet with BPM and duration in tabular figures; `/builds` leads with a photograph of the actual $10K award cheque.
- Two self-hosted ITF variable faces (Clash Display + Switzer) instead of the Google grotesques every generated portfolio reaches for — and no mono, with `tabular-nums` doing the numeric-alignment job instead.
- One ambient motion (`WaveField`) with everything else deriving from a single BPM-92 grid. The repo documents having *built and deleted* two pinned scroll sequences and a whole drawn-line background system. Restraint that was earned by removal is the opposite of generated.

What is generic: the icon-only desktop rail, and the fact that `/music` and `/lens` are alphabetical/undated lists — neither carries authorship the way the hero and the contact sheet do.

**Deterministic scan:** `detect.mjs --json app` → `[]`, exit 0, both before and after the fix batch. Zero findings, zero false positives to triage. Browser evidence corroborated: 0 CLS on all five routes, 0 console errors, 0 failed requests, 0 missing alt, exactly one `h1` per route with no skipped levels, 0 horizontal overflow across 30 route×viewport combinations plus a 200% zoom pass.

**Visual overlays:** not attempted. A dev server was already running for five parallel agents and the playbook's overlay flow requires starting `live-server.mjs`; starting a second server would have broken the siblings. No user-visible overlay exists — reported as fallback rather than claimed.

## Overall Impression

The craft floor here is genuinely high, and the defects were not where a normal audit looks. Nothing was broken in the layout, the tokens, or the motion system — those are close to exemplary. What was broken was **credibility detail**: a duplicated word in the featured project's first sentence, a decode effect that rendered the candidate's own job title as a plausible misspelling every four seconds, tempo and duration silently dropped on the device most visitors use, and a conventions document mandating an entire design system the site does not have.

The single biggest opportunity is not visual. It is that the hero's own copy contradicts the site's stated positioning — see P1 below.

## What's Working

1. **Copy discipline in the contact form.** Every confirmation branch claims exactly what the code proved: `res.ok` says "the server accepted", the `mailto` branch says "should have opened", and the icon changes to match the sentence. This is the rarest thing in the audit and it is done better here than in most production software.
2. **Contrast engineered by role.** `--accent` (3.87:1) is scoped to shapes and `--accent-text` (4.93:1) is required for words — and grep confirms the split is honoured at every callsite. Three text greys, all clearing 4.5:1.
3. **The `/lens` contact sheet.** A justified grid with real capture data under each frame lets the artifact lead and the interface recede, which is exactly right for an Experience surface.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The hero copy demotes two of the three pillars to hobbies.** `app/lib/components/home/Hero.tsx` renders "Exploring music production, photography, and video games in my free time." PRODUCT.md's positioning is explicit that the three disciplines are "one identity rather than a job plus two hobbies" and that nav weight is "a structural commitment, not a tagline". The hero says the opposite of the structure. "Video games" also has no artifact anywhere on the site, against the stated principle that every claim has one; and "entrepreneurial award winner" is vaguer than the concrete fact available (2nd place, $10K, Cal Poly Innovation Quest). *Not changed — this is factual/positioning copy and needs the owner's call.* → `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1 — fixed] Decode effect rendered the job title as a typo.** `SCRAMBLE_CHARS` was 65% alphabetic, so mid-decode frames read as misspelled real words: captured "Full-Stack Develoler" in the hero and "V488rMC56L" for the Experience heading, looping every 4s forever on the primary conversion surface. Glyph set is now digits and symbols only; verified 45/48 mid-decode frames carry a non-letter glyph and zero frames are all-letter misspellings.

**[P1 — fixed] Mobile `/music` dropped the two figures that matter.** `TrackRow.tsx:114` hid BPM and duration below `lg` while the description kept its row and truncated mid-word — and the `aria-label` was already announcing the tempo, so screen-reader users got a figure sighted mobile visitors could not see. Both now ride the title row on mobile.

**[P1 — fixed] Weak focus indicator on the résumé download, in both places it appears.** `outline-none` with a 1px `border-accent` swap as the only cue, on one of the two conversions the site exists for. Now a real 2px `outline-accent` at 4px offset, verified computed.

**[P1 — fixed] `AGENTS.md` mandated a design system that does not exist.** The documented palette (`#0d6efd`, `#2b2b2b`, `#fbfbfb`), fonts (`font-domine`, `font-ibm`), card recipe (`rounded-xl shadow-lg`), and a gradient-pill skill-tag string prescribed "everywhere for consistency" (carrying `transition-all` and `hover:scale-105`) appear **only** in that file — zero matches anywhere in `app/`. It also falsely claimed `OrbitStage` is never mounted, which caused two independent reviewers to file live code as dead. A conventions doc that contradicts the code is a defect generator; rewritten against the implementation.

## Persona Red Flags

**The recruiter with 30 seconds (arriving from a résumé link, on a phone):** the fact grid — Currently building / Studying / Based in / Seeking, plus the Résumé button — is the highest-signal block on the site and on mobile it sits roughly 1.5 screens down, behind a five-line paragraph and a portrait. The paragraph they read *instead* is the one that calls two of the three disciplines a free-time activity. They also pay a 2.6s loading gate before seeing anything.

**Sam (screen reader + keyboard):** now has a skip link (there was none — every route required tabbing the five nav items first). The `RoleCycle` scramble was the one place mid-decode glyphs could become the accessible text; it now carries the settled role in `sr-only` with the churn `aria-hidden`, matching what `ManifestoHeading` already did correctly. Project icon links were 19×19 (WCAG 2.5.8 wants 24×24) and are now 27×27 via a hit-area extender, with no visual change.

**Casey (one-handed mobile):** BPM/duration now visible; no horizontal overflow at 320/390; the transport correctly reserves its footprint inside the footer so it never occludes links.

## Minor Observations

- `public/beats/the_bag_2.mp3` is on disk but referenced by no `Beat` entry — orphaned asset, left in place pending the owner's intent (it may be an unreleased take).
- Required-field errors on `/reach` fall back to Chrome's native bubble ("Please fill out this field"), which is not the site's plain-factual voice and is the one place the form's otherwise excellent copy discipline lapses.
- `/lens` throws a single 233ms frame during a full-page scroll (image decode as a contact-sheet row enters); median and p95 are 16.7ms on every route.
- The desktop sidebar animates `width` rather than a transform. It is `position: fixed`, so the reflow is confined to its own subtree and no jank was measured — left alone deliberately rather than refactored on principle.
- `/music` and `/lens` are the two surfaces with no authored ordering: an alphabetical beat list and an undated contact sheet. Neither carries the editorial intent the rest of the site does.

## Questions to Consider
