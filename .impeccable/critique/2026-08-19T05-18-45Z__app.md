## Design Specificity Verdict

## What is working

- Accent is reserved for shapes; readable blue text uses the dedicated accent-text token.
- The gallery has redundant, keyboard-safe exit paths and focus return.
- WaveField remains the sole ambient layer and avoids competing background systems.

## Constraint review

ContactForm intentionally uses Formspree `mode: "no-cors"`; a fulfilled request is the established success condition because browser-readable HTTP status is unavailable under that endpoint contract. This is documented project behavior, not changed in this pass.

## Remaining observation

The justified gallery intentionally renders after its client width measurement. It is documented to seed synchronously on mount and avoids malformed zero-width rows; no change is warranted without a server-compatible layout strategy.
