## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored for this product, unevenly.** Verdict: pass.

**LLM assessment.** The specificity is real and lives in places a template could not reach: `RoleCycle` hand-sequences its roles so no two non-engineering roles sit adjacent, which is a decision that exists only because "range" is the claim; `RouteSignal` draws five genuinely distinct SVG paths rather than reusing one icon; `PlayerBar`'s fill breathes at the *track's* BPM while every other entrance on the site derives from the BPM-92 grid in `app/lib/tempo.ts` — a music-production identity baked structurally into `/builds` and `/reach`, which have nothing to do with music. The transport surviving route navigation (verified live: audio stayed unpaused across `/music` → `/lens`) is the one interaction that demonstrates multi-discipline coexistence instead of asserting it.

Weakest: `/lens`. `JustifiedGrid` + `GalleryCell` is a well-executed editorial contact sheet, but nothing in its chrome signals it is one third of an engineer's range — it would ship unchanged on an unrelated photographer's site. It has zero connective tissue to the other two disciplines beyond the ambient `WaveField` and `RouteSignal`, both already global. A visitor arriving cold from a shared photo link has no signal they landed inside a larger claim.

Structural sameness across `/builds`, `/music`, `/reach` at the header level (`pt-16` → display-lg h1 → `RouteSignal`) is a documented consistency device, not drift — but it means all product character has to live in the page bodies, and two of those three bodies are plain lists.

**Deterministic scan.** `detect.mjs --json app`: exit 0, **zero findings**. The in-page overlay pass found four rule classes: `dark-glow` (2× on every route), `cramped-padding` (5 on `/`, 1 each on `/builds`/`/music`/`/reach`), `ai-color-palette` (1 on `/lens`), `image-hover-transform` (1 on `/reach`, citing 4 images).

Adjudication after verification in source:
- `dark-glow` — **false positive.** It resolves to `SignalRule`'s `box-shadow: 0 6px 14px -7px rgb(var(--accent-rgb) / …)`, which has a real 6px offset and blur; it is not the zero-offset halo the rule targets. Documented in `AGENTS.md` as one of three sanctioned shadows.
- `ai-color-palette` "purple/violet gradient" on `/lens` — **true positive, and B mis-scored it as noise.** B dismissed it because the element measured 0×0.98px, but the element is the Lens header rule's accent segment and it genuinely carried two hard-coded hexes (`#5575d9`, `#8b66c9`) outside the documented palette. The colours are user-pinned, so the correct fix is a token, not a hue change.
- `cramped-padding` on `/`'s skill chips — **true positive.** `ExperienceList`'s chips use `py-1` (4px) under 14px text.
- `image-hover-transform` on `/reach` — **true positive but low value.** The montage images carry `group-hover:scale-[1.025]`; `AGENTS.md` bans hover zoom on `/lens` thumbnails specifically, so this is an inconsistency between two photo surfaces rather than a defect.

**Measured evidence (no design judgment).** Zero horizontal overflow on 5 routes × 3 widths, including the historically fragile 800px band. Zero contrast violations below 4.5:1 once the measurement harness was corrected for Tailwind v4's `oklab()` output. Zero instances of `--accent` used on text. Zero console errors across 15 route/width combinations. Zero broken images once native `loading="lazy"` false positives were excluded. Zero `aria-hidden` subtrees containing focusable nodes. `will-change` unused everywhere. Reduced motion correctly suppresses the loader and renders a byte-identical static `WaveField` frame; `prefers-contrast: more` correctly paints the transport solid `rgb(0,0,0)` with `backdrop-filter: none`.

**Visual overlays.** Injection succeeded and the overlay ran in-page across five routes; the bundled `detect.js` exposes `window.impeccableDetectAsync()` rather than logging, so results came from calling that API directly — there is no `impeccable`-prefixed console output by design. The live server was stopped and the port confirmed closed.

## Overall Impression

This is a genuinely well-built site whose defects are all at the edges rather than in the concept. The restraint claims in `AGENTS.md` are not aspirational — they are actually enforced in the rendered result, which is rare. The single biggest opportunity is that the two surfaces carrying two thirds of the "range" claim (`/music`, `/lens`) are structurally the thinnest: both have literally no heading below the h1, and `/lens` has no connective tissue to the rest of the identity at all. The second is that every remaining P1 lands on `/reach` — the one page whose entire job is conversion.

## What's Working

1. **The transport persists, and the architecture was bent to make it.** `PlayerBar` renders through `createPortal` specifically because the route wrapper's `clip-path` transition would otherwise clip a `position: fixed` element. That is a real decision made to protect one cross-route interaction, and it is the strongest "this person ships working software" proof on the site because it is an interaction, not a screenshot.
2. **Error copy in the product's own voice.** "Add an email address — there's no way to reply without one" instead of "Invalid email." One detail doing more for the plain-factual voice mandate than any headline.
3. **The hairline-only surface discipline survives audit.** Across 15 route×width captures, no drop-shadow-as-elevation and no rounded card appeared outside the three documented exceptions.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] `/reach` opens a 179px dead band at tablet width
**Why it matters** — Measured at 800×1000: form column bottom 792px, last photo bottom 613px. 72px of that is self-inflicted by `md:pt-[4.5rem]` pushing the *shorter* column down. It lands on the page whose only job is converting attention into contact, at the width a recruiter on a part-width laptop window actually uses.
**Fix** — Apply the photo column's top offset only from `lg` up, where that column is the taller of the two; keep the two-column split at `md`.
**Suggested command** — `/impeccable layout`

### [P1] Contact form withholds validation until submit
**Why it matters** — `emailProblem()` runs only inside `submit`. A recruiter filling the form once, fast, learns their address is malformed only after committing. Heuristic 9.
**Fix** — Validate on blur, but only for a field the visitor actually filled, reusing the existing error strings and `aria-invalid`/`aria-describedby` wiring. Empty fields stay the submit gate's job so tabbing through cannot nag.
**Suggested command** — `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] `SK` wordmark fails WCAG 2.2 target size
**Why it matters** — Measured 20.39×24.61 CSS px on all five routes at tablet and mobile. Under the 24px SC 2.5.8 AA floor on width, and no inline-text exception applies to a standalone nav control. The sibling nav links in the same bar already solve this with `min-h-11`.
**Fix** — Give the link the same `inline-flex min-h-11 items-center` treatment, compensating padding so the glyphs do not shift.
**Suggested command** — `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] `/music` and `/lens` have no heading below the h1
**Why it matters** — Both outlines are exactly one `h1`. `/builds` has 18 headings. A screen-reader user who arrived directly at `/music` from a shared beat link or `/lens` from a shared photo — an explicitly stated secondary persona — has nothing to navigate to.
**Fix** — Promote the existing unnamed regions (`/music`'s filter group and track list; `/lens`'s contact sheet) to `section aria-labelledby` pointing at `sr-only` `h2`s, converting the existing `aria-label` strings rather than inventing copy.
**Suggested command** — `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] Featured project's evidence is illegible on mobile
**Why it matters** — With a `secondary` image the container is `grid-cols-2` at `aspectRatio: 3`, so at 390px each screenshot renders about 170×145px. A dev-tool UI screenshot is unreadable at that size, so a mobile visitor gets the weakest view of the one project the design promotes as featured.
**Fix** — Stack the pair full-width below `sm:` at `aspect-[16/10]` (the ratio `ProjectCard` already uses), keep the side-by-side composition from `sm:` up, and correct the now-wrong `sizes` hint.
**Suggested command** — `/impeccable adapt`

## Persona Red Flags

**Recruiter with 30 seconds** — The hero plus `GridIndex` delivers "range" inside the first viewport; that is the strongest five seconds on the site. But following through to `/reach` at a part-width window lands them on the 179px dead band exactly as they decide whether to email — the worst-timed defect on the site for the primary persona.

**Riley (mobile, one-handed)** — Gets the illegible featured screenshots, and separately loses `GridIndex`'s one-line route descriptors, which are `sr-only` below `sm:`. Riley chooses between "Builds" and "Lens" from bare labels at exactly the width where a descriptor helps most.

**Sam (screen reader / keyboard only)** — The deliberate work holds: tab order is logical and complete (`Skip to content → Home → Builds → Music → Lens → Reach → Résumé`), focus rings are present and high contrast, Escape and arrow keys drive the lightbox, no `aria-hidden` subtree contains a focusable node. Two real gaps: `/music` and `/lens` offer no headings to jump to, and `RoleCycle`'s `sr-only` span emits whichever single role was current when the reader reached it, with no live region — so the cycling that carries the range claim for sighted users is silently invisible to Sam.

## Minor Observations

- `Footer`'s links use an 8px `::before` hit-area extender — a considerate pattern worth auditing `TrackRow`'s play control against.
- `/builds` paints 10 elements at `blur(64px)`. Verified as `ProjectCard`'s cursor-proximity glow, capped at 0.06 opacity and 0 at rest — intentional, not drift.
- `ExperienceList` chips are bordered pills while `ProjectSkills` on `/builds` is `/`-divided text. Two treatments for the same content type across two routes.
- The `/lens` lightbox scrim still shows the sheet faintly behind it — correct per the documented defocus intent, but softer than "deliberately darker than the page" reads on paper.
- `ProjectMeta`'s icon links are 19×19 raw with a `before:-inset-1` slop reaching ~27×27 — clears the 24px AA floor but falls short of the 44px pattern `PlayerBar` establishes elsewhere in the same codebase.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the persistent transport is the site's best proof of engineering craft *in use*, why does nothing outside `/music` ever invite a visitor to start a track before they leave?
2. Given this project's own history of tablet-width regressions, is a single `lg` cutoff still the right model, or does the content want a genuinely tuned mid-width tier?
3. What is the one artifact — a caption, a cross-link, a shared subject — that would make a visitor landing cold on `/lens` realise they are inside a bigger story?
4. The contact CTA is a `mailto:` handoff. Has that been exercised on a locked-down work laptop with no configured mail client, which is exactly the primary persona's machine?
